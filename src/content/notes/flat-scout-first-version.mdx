## What happened

In late September, the first version of flat scout ran on the Haiku model and automated the whole outreach, not only the search.<Ref id="E1" /> It searched one page of one rental portal.<Ref id="E2" /> It missed major sources of listings.<Ref id="E3" /> Its scheduled task was bound to my laptop, and did not run while the laptop slept.<Ref id="E4" /> I rated it unacceptable and ordered a full rebuild.<Ref id="E5" />

## Causes

1. The search was narrow.<Ref id="E2" /> It covered one page of one portal,<Ref id="E2" /> and it missed major sources.<Ref id="E3" /> I read the first as a reason for the second.<Ref id="E2" /><Ref id="E3" />
2. The schedule depended on a laptop that was awake.<Ref id="E4" /> When the laptop slept, the task did not run.<Ref id="E4" />

## What changed

- I ordered a full rebuild.<Ref id="E5" />
- The second version was search-only.<Ref id="E6" />
- It paginated its searches.<Ref id="E18" />
- It had a coverage dashboard.<Ref id="E19" />
- It ran on the Opus model, where the first version had run on Haiku.<Ref id="E1" /><Ref id="E6" />

## What is still not known

<ul className="unknown">
<li>Whether coverage improved, and if it did, which change did it. The rebuild changed the model, the search and the reporting at the same time.</li>
<li>Whether the model mattered. The first version ran on a small model. I did not test the same design on a larger one, or the old model with the new paging.</li>
<li>How many listings the first version missed. The record says "major sources" and holds no count.</li>
<li>When the schedule moved off the laptop. The third version ran as scheduled cloud sessions. The record does not say that move answered this cause.</li>
<li>How many scheduled runs the sleeping laptop cost. The record holds no count of missed runs.</li>
<li>Whether automating the whole outreach counted in my rating, or only the coverage.</li>
<li>The day of each event. The record dates the first version to a month.</li>
<li>When the fixes were made. The rows for them carry the month of the second version's first run.</li>
</ul>
