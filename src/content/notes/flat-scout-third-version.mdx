## What happened

From <Fig row="E9">19 to 26 October</Fig>, the scheduled cloud sessions of the third version of flat scout, which ran on the Sonnet model, lacked the web-fetch tool.<Ref id="E9" /> The scheduled runs failed silently or logged failed immediately.<Ref id="E10" /> During that week I paused the automation and asked for manual scans instead.<Ref id="E11" />

## Causes

1. The scheduled cloud sessions did not have the web-fetch tool.<Ref id="E9" /> I read that as the reason the runs failed.<Ref id="E9" /><Ref id="E10" />
2. The runs failed silently, or logged a failure at once.<Ref id="E10" /> The record does not say which, or whether I read a logged failure.<Ref id="E10" />

## What changed

- I paused the automation and asked for manual scans instead.<Ref id="E11" />
- Flat scout gained a first step that checks that the tools it needs are loaded.<Ref id="E24" />
- When a required tool is absent, that step writes a failed entry and stops.<Ref id="E24" />

## What is still not known

<ul className="unknown">
<li>Why the scheduled cloud sessions lacked the web-fetch tool. The record says only that they lacked it.</li>
<li>Which of the two failures happened, and how often. "Failed silently" and "logged failed immediately" are different events. In one, nobody is told. In the other, a failed entry exists and may go unread. The record joins them in one phrase.</li>
<li>Whether the agent reported the missing tool and I missed the report.</li>
<li>Whether the lack of a first step that checks for the tools was a cause. The step was added; nothing tested it, and some runs may already have logged a failure.</li>
<li>How long the failures went unnoticed before I paused the automation.</li>
<li>How many scheduled runs were lost. The record holds no count.</li>
<li>Whether the first step has caught a missing tool since. The record shows that the step was added, not that it has fired.</li>
<li>When the first step was added. The row for it carries the month of the failure, not the month of the fix.</li>
</ul>
